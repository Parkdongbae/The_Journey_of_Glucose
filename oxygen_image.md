# Image Asset List for "Journey of Oxygen" Web App
# Filename: oxygen_image.md

This document lists image assets needed for the **“Journey of Oxygen”** course and provides **English prompts** suitable for generating each image using GPT Image2.  
The visual level must match the Korean middle-school science textbook (unit: “Animals and Energy” – respiration, circulation, excretion, and cellular respiration).

---

## 1. Shared Front Page Images (Course Selection)

### 1-1. Course Selection Screen – Glucose vs Oxygen

**Description (Korean)**  
- 첫 화면에 “Journey of Glucose”와 “Journey of Oxygen” 두 코스를 보여주는 일러스트.
- 왼쪽에는 포도당 캐릭터, 오른쪽에는 산소 캐릭터를 배치.
- 배경에 간단한 인체 실루엣이나 세포 아이콘을 넣어, 우리 몸 여행 느낌을 전달.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A bright, friendly course selection illustration for a middle-school science web app. Show two large cards side by side: one labeled “Journey of Glucose” with a cute glucose character (yellow-orange round face), and one labeled “Journey of Oxygen” with a cute oxygen character (light blue round face). Use a simple human silhouette or small cells in the background to suggest a journey through the body. Flat colorful style, classroom-safe, no realistic medical detail.

## 2. Respiratory System Images (Oxygen Path)

### 2-1. Basic Respiratory System Diagram (Front View)

**Description (Korean)**  
- 코, 숨관, 숨관가지, 폐, 가로막을 포함하는 기본 호흡계 모식도. 【2†source】  
- 공기가 코에서 폐로 들어가는 들숨 경로를 화살표로 표현.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified front-view diagram of the human respiratory system for middle-school students. Show the nose, trachea, bronchi, lungs, and diaphragm, with arrows indicating air flowing from the nose down the trachea and bronchi into the lungs. Use flat colors and clear shapes, textbook-style, no realistic textures.

---

### 2-2. Side-view Respiratory Path – Nose to Lungs

**Description (Korean)**  
- 측면 인체에서 코 → 숨관 → 숨관가지 → 폐로 이어지는 공기 통로를 강조한 그림.
- 산소 아이콘이 경로를 따라 이동하는 모습을 나중에 애니메이션으로 사용.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified side-view illustration of the upper body showing the path of air from the nose through the trachea and bronchi into the lungs. Highlight the airway in a contrasting color and leave space to overlay small oxygen icons moving along the path. Flat, educational style.

---

### 2-3. Diaphragm Movement – Inhalation and Exhalation

**Description (Korean)**  
- 들숨/날숨 시 갈비뼈와 가로막의 움직임을 비교하는 두 패널 또는 하나의 그림.
- 들숨: 갈비뼈 올라감, 가로막 내려감, 흉강 부피 증가.
- 날숨: 갈비뼈 내려감, 가로막 올라감, 흉강 부피 감소. 【2†source】  

**Prompt (English)**  
> An educational diagram comparing inhalation and exhalation for middle-school students. Show the rib cage and diaphragm in two states: inhalation (ribs elevated, diaphragm flattened downward, chest cavity larger) and exhalation (ribs lowered, diaphragm domed upward, chest cavity smaller). Use arrows and labels to indicate volume and pressure changes, in a flat, schematic style.

**Prompt (English)**

## 5. Cellular Respiration and Waste Images

### 5-1. Cellular Respiration Focused on Oxygen

**Description (Korean)**  
- 세포 안에서 포도당 + 산소 → 이산화 탄소 + 물 + 에너지 식을 시각적으로 표현하되, 산소 역할을 강조. 【2†source】  
- 산소 아이콘이 포도당과 함께 세포 속으로 들어가고, 에너지 아이콘과 노폐물 아이콘이 나오는 그림.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A concept illustration of cellular respiration focused on oxygen for middle-school students. Show a cell with a cute glucose character and oxygen icons entering, and on the other side display icons for carbon dioxide, water drops, and an energy symbol (lightning bolt). Include the equation “Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy” in clear text. Flat, playful style.

---

### 5-2. Carbon Dioxide Leaving via Lungs

**Description (Korean)**  
- 세포에서 생성된 이산화 탄소가 혈액을 통해 폐로 돌아가, 허파꽈리에서 공기 쪽으로 나간 뒤 날숨으로 밖으로 나가는 과정 개념도.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A schematic “journey of carbon dioxide” illustration for middle-school science. Show a body cell producing CO2, CO2 icons traveling in the blood to the lungs, entering alveoli, and then moving out through the airways to be exhaled. Use arrows to show direction and keep the style flat and educational.

---

### 5-3. Water and Wastes Leaving via Kidneys

**Description (Korean)**  
- 세포호흡으로 생성된 물과 요소 등이 혈액을 통해 콩팥으로 이동해, 여과·재흡수·분비 과정을 거쳐 오줌으로 나가는 과정 모식도. 【2†source】  

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified diagram connecting cellular respiration to kidney excretion for middle-school students. Show water and waste icons traveling in the blood to the kidneys, then passing through a nephron diagram (filtration, reabsorption, secretion) and finally leaving as urine. Use flat colors and simple arrows.
