# Image Asset List for "Journey of Glucose" Web App
# Filename: image.md

This document lists the image assets needed for the **"Journey of Glucose"** web app and provides **English prompts** suitable for generating each image using GPT Image2.  
All images must stay within the conceptual level of the Korean middle-school science textbook (unit: "Animals and Energy" – digestion, circulation, respiration, excretion, and cellular respiration).

---

## 1. Global / Layout Images

### 1-1. Main Title Screen – “Journey of Glucose”

**Description (Korean)**  
- 웹앱의 첫 화면으로 사용할 메인 일러스트.  
- 배경에 간단한 인체 실루엣이 있고, 가운데에는 “Journey of Glucose” 타이틀과 귀여운 포도당 캐릭터가 있음.  
- 학교 과학 수업용이므로 밝고 친근한 느낌, 과도한 의학적 디테일 없이 단순화된 스타일.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A bright, friendly main illustration for a middle-school science web app titled “Journey of Glucose”. Show a simplified human silhouette in the background and a cute glucose character (small yellow-orange sphere with a simple smiling face) in the center near the title text “Journey of Glucose”. The overall style should be flat, colorful, and classroom-friendly, with no realistic gore or medical detail, just simple schematic shapes.

## 2. Digestive System Images

### 2-1. Basic Digestive Tract Diagram

**Description (Korean)**  
- 입, 식도, 위, 작은창자, 큰창자, 항문을 한 눈에 볼 수 있는 기본 소화관 모식도.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified colored diagram of the human digestive system for middle-school science. Show the mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, anus, and also indicate the salivary glands, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas. The organs should be drawn in a schematic textbook style, with clear labels area space, no realistic textures, just flat shapes and soft colors.

**Description (Korean)**

**Prompt (English)**  
> A close-up schematic illustration of the interior of the small intestine for middle-school science. Show folds and fingerlike villi, with each villus containing a small network of capillaries and a central lacteal. The style should be clean and educational, using simple labels spaces and flat colors, no microscopic realistic detail.

## 3. Circulatory System Images

### 3-1. Heart and Major Blood Vessels Diagram

**Description (Korean)**  
- 우심방, 우심실, 좌심방, 좌심실이 구분된 심장 모식도.  
- 대동맥, 대정맥, 폐동맥, 폐정맥 등 주요 혈관을 중학교 수준으로 표시.  
- 허파순환과 온몸순환 경로를 색으로 구분할 수 있도록 한다.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified diagram of the human heart and major blood vessels for middle-school science. Clearly show the right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium, and left ventricle, along with the aorta, vena cava, pulmonary artery, and pulmonary vein. Use different colors to suggest pulmonary circulation vs systemic circulation. Keep the style schematic and textbook-like, without anatomical realism.

---

### 3-2. Blood Circulation Path – Pulmonary and Systemic

**Description (Korean)**  
- 심장에서 폐로 가는 허파순환과 심장에서 온몸으로 가는 온몸순환을 한 그림에 표현.  
- 각 순환 경로를 굵은 화살표와 색으로 표현하여 학생들이 흐름을 쉽게 이해할 수 있게 함.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A schematic diagram showing pulmonary circulation and systemic circulation for middle-school students. Use a simplified heart icon in the center, lungs on one side, and the body on the other side. Draw bold arrows in different colors to show blood flow from the heart to the lungs and back, and from the heart to the body and back. The style should be clean, flat, and easy to read.

---

### 3-3. Blood Vessel Cross-section – Capillary Exchange

**Description (Korean)**  
- 모세혈관 단면에서 혈액과 조직세포 사이에 산소, 이산화 탄소, 영양소, 노폐물 교환이 일어나는 모습을 개념적으로 표현.  
- 포도당과 산소가 세포로 들어가고, 이산화 탄소와 노폐물이 혈액 쪽으로 나오는 화살표를 사용.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A cross-sectional schematic of a capillary and a nearby body cell for middle-school science. Show arrows indicating oxygen and glucose moving from blood to the cell, and arrows for carbon dioxide and wastes moving from the cell back into the blood. Use simple icons and flat colors to represent substances, avoiding realistic histology.

### 5-2. Nephron (Kidney Unit) – Filtration and Reabsorption

**Description (Korean)**  
- 토리(사구체), 보먼주머니, 세뇨관을 포함한 콩팥단위 구조 모식도.  
- 여과, 재흡수, 분비 과정 방향을 화살표로 표시하고, 포도당이 재흡수되는 개념을 쉽게 표현.

Use arrows to indicate filtration from the glomerulus into Bowman’s capsule, reabsorption from the tubule back into blood, and secretion into the tubule. Include a simple icon or label indicating that glucose is reabsorbed, in a flat, educational style.

## 6. Cellular Respiration & Integrated Concept Images

### 6-1. Cellular Respiration Equation Illustration

**Description (Korean)**  
- 세포 호흡 식 `포도당 + 산소 → 이산화 탄소 + 물 + 에너지`를 시각적으로 보여주는 그림.  
- 포도당 캐릭터와 산소 아이콘이 세포 안으로 들어가고, 이산화 탄소·물·에너지 아이콘이 나오는 개념.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A concept illustration of cellular respiration for middle-school students. Show a cute glucose character and an oxygen icon entering a simplified cell, and on the other side, show icons for carbon dioxide, water drops, and an energy symbol (such as a glowing lightning bolt). Include the equation “Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy” in clear text. Use a flat, playful style.

---

### 6-2. Integrated Pathway – Journey of Glucose Map

**Description (Korean)**  
- 떡볶이(녹말)에서 시작해 소화계→순환계→호흡계→배설계→세포호흡으로 이어지는 전체 흐름을 하나의 지도처럼 표현한 그림.  
- 각 기관계는 다른 색으로 표현하고, 포도당 캐릭터가 경로를 따라 여행하는 모습.

**Prompt (English)**  
> A simplified “journey map” of glucose for middle-school science. Start with a cartoon rice cake (starch), then show the digestive system, circulatory system, respiratory system, excretory system, and a cell representing cellular respiration, arranged in sequence. Draw a cute glucose character traveling along arrows through each system. Use different background colors for each system to distinguish them, keeping the style flat and educational.
